# Art credits

All 3D models, textures and the outdoor HDRI come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)
and are released under CC0 (public domain). `assets_manifest.py` lists every model used.
Textures: laminate_floor_02, herringbone_parquet, floor_tiles_06, long_white_tiles,
marble_01, kitchen_wood, fabric_pattern_07, oak_veneer_01. HDRI: symmetrical_garden_02.
Rooms are original layouts built with `build_rooms.py` and rendered in Blender.
