# Art credits

All 3D models, textures and the outdoor HDRIs come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)
and are released under CC0 (public domain). `assets_manifest.py` lists every model,
texture and HDRI used (`ALL_MODELS`, `TEXTURES`, `HDRIS`).
HDRIs: symmetrical_garden_02 (garage, hallway), cloudy_vondelpark (living room, bedroom).
Rooms are original layouts built with `build_rooms.py` and rendered in Blender.
Sinks, taps, cabinets, the flat TV, curtains, sockets, the shoe rack and the coat rail
are simple geometry built in `build_rooms.py`, as are the bedroom mattress and blanket,
the window blind, the garage pegboard and the strip light.
